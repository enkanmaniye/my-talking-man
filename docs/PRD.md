1. Product Overview
1.1 Product Vision
My Talking Man is an interactive virtual companion application that allows users to create a personalized 3D digital version of their boyfriend.
The application combines the gameplay mechanics of traditional virtual pet games with generative AI, 3D character modeling, and persistent conversational memory.
Users begin by uploading photographs of their boyfriend from multiple angles. AI processes these photographs to generate a personalized 3D male character that resembles the person in the uploaded pictures.
After generating the 3D model, the application conducts an interactive onboarding interview to learn about the boyfriend's appearance, personality, interests, preferences, life experiences, ambitions, dreams, and dislikes.
This information becomes the foundation of the character's personality and behavioral model.
The resulting character is not simply a static 3D avatar. He has a simulated personality, individual preferences, emotional states, needs, and long-term memory.
Users can interact with him, talk to him, play games, take care of his needs, purchase clothes and furniture, decorate his environment, and gradually develop their virtual companion.
1.2 Core Product Concept
The application consists of three interconnected systems:
1. Virtual Pet Simulation
Inspired by the gameplay mechanics of Talking Tom, the application features a 3D character with needs such as hunger, health, energy, and sleep. Users take care of him and maintain his well-being.
2. AI Personality and Memory
An AI system creates the character's personality based on the user's onboarding answers. The character remembers relevant information and uses it to influence his conversations and behavior.
3. Virtual Life and Customization
Users can build their character's virtual lifestyle by purchasing clothes, food, furniture, accessories, and other items. They can earn virtual currency through activities and mini-games.

Required photograph categories:

•	Left-side profile.

•	Three-quarter view.

Examples:

Discover what the character dislikes or avoids.
Examples:

•	What situations make him uncomfortable?
•	What kind of people does he dislike?
•	What topics does he avoid?

Category G: Ambitions and Dreams

Understand how the boyfriend behaves in a romantic relationship.

4.4 Personality Generation
Once the interview is complete, the AI processes all collected information and creates a structured personality profile.
The profile should contain:
•	Core personality traits.
•	Communication style.
•	Humor style.
•	Emotional tendencies.
•	Interests and hobbies.
•	Likes and dislikes.
•	Food preferences.
•	Important life experiences.
•	Ambitions and goals.
•	Relationship preferences.
•	Behavioral patterns.
The system then creates a persistent character profile that is used by the conversational AI.
Important: The character should not simply repeat information provided during onboarding. It should use that information to generate contextually appropriate conversations, preferences, reactions, and behaviors.
The AI must treat the profile as a user-provided fictional simulation, not as verified knowledge of the real person's thoughts or feelings.

7. AI Conversation System
This is one of the application's core differentiating features.
Users should be able to communicate with their virtual boyfriend through a dedicated chat interface.
7.1 Conversational AI
The character should communicate in natural language.
The AI must use:
•	The generated personality profile.
•	Personal interests.
•	Communication preferences.
•	Important life experiences.
•	Previous conversations.
•	Relevant long-term memories.
•	Current game state.
The character should maintain a consistent personality across conversations.
For example:
If the boyfriend is described as sarcastic, ambitious, introverted, and interested in technology, the character should naturally reflect those characteristics when communicating.
7.2 Persistent Memory
The AI should remember relevant information from previous interactions.
Memory should operate on two levels:
Long-term memory:
•	Personality characteristics.
•	Important personal experiences.
•	Interests.
•	Preferences.
•	Ambitions.
•	Important facts established during conversations.
Short-term conversational memory:
•	Current conversation.
•	Recent topics.
•	Temporary emotional context.
•	Recently discussed activities.
The character should be able to refer to previously discussed topics naturally.
Example:
If the user previously discussed his favorite movie, the character may mention that movie in a later conversation.
The AI should avoid inventing personal experiences or presenting fictional memories as actual events.
Users should be able to review, edit, and delete stored memories.
7.3 Personality Consistency
The character should not behave like a generic AI assistant.
Its responses must reflect the personality profile.
The AI should control:
•	Vocabulary.
•	Sentence structure.
•	Humor.
•	Emotional expressiveness.
•	Conversational energy.
•	Interests.
•	Preferences.
•	Typical reactions.
The personality should remain stable while allowing the character to respond naturally to new situations.

17. Key Product Principles
The following principles should guide all product and design decisions:
1.	Personalization first: The character must reflect the specific person described by the user rather than behave like a generic virtual pet.
2.	Personality consistency: The character's behavior, dialogue, and preferences must be consistent with its generated personality profile.
3.	Persistent memory: The character should remember relevant information across multiple sessions.
4.	Interactive gameplay: The character must be more than a chatbot. The 3D simulation and virtual pet mechanics are essential.
5.	Visual engagement: The 3D character should remain the center of the application.
6.	Progression and rewards: Users should have reasons to return, play games, and customize their character.
7.	User control: Users should be able to edit the character's personality, memories, and appearance.
8.	Privacy by design: The application must respect the privacy and likeness rights of the person being represented.

For example:
If the character is configured as sarcastic, introverted, intellectually curious, and slightly cold, he should not suddenly behave like an extremely enthusiastic and affectionate generic chatbot.
However, the character should still be capable of adapting his tone to different situations.
The memory system should preserve important information from conversations and make it available in future interactions.
Users should be able to:
•	View saved memories.
•	Edit memories.
•	Delete memories.
•	Correct inaccurate information.
•	Add important information manually.
•	Reset the character's personality if desired.
The AI must distinguish between facts supplied by the user, fictional character memories, and information generated during roleplay. It must not claim that simulated experiences are verified facts about the real boyfriend.
7. Integration with the Virtual Pet Simulation
The conversational AI and virtual pet simulation should operate as interconnected systems.
The character's current state can influence how he communicates.
Examples:
•	When tired, he may give shorter responses or mention wanting to sleep.
•	When hungry, he may bring up food.
•	When happy, he may communicate more enthusiastically.
•	When interacting with a favorite object, he may express excitement.
•	When receiving a gift he dislikes, he may react sarcastically or humorously.
The character's personality determines how he expresses these states.
For example, two characters with different personalities should react differently to the same situation.
A romantic character might say something affectionate, while a sarcastic character might make a joke.
The virtual pet mechanics must not unnecessarily interrupt free conversation. Users should always retain access to the chat.
8. Updated Main Navigation
The main application should have the following navigation:
1.	Home: Interactive 3D boyfriend and virtual environment.
2.	Chat: Unlimited free-form conversations with the AI character.
3.	Games: Mini-games and activities.
4.	Shop: Food, clothing, furniture, and accessories.
5.	Profile / Settings: Personality customization, topic preferences, memories, and application settings.
9. Updated MVP Requirements
Free-form AI conversation is a mandatory MVP feature, not a future enhancement.
The first release must include:
•	Text-based free conversation.
•	Configurable personality traits.
•	Configurable communication styles.
•	Topic likes and dislikes.
•	Personality-conditioned AI responses.
•	Persistent conversation history.
•	Basic long-term memory.
•	Ability to edit personality settings.
•	Integration between conversational personality and virtual pet state.
Voice conversations, advanced voice personalization, and more sophisticated emotional animations can be introduced in later versions.
10. Core Product Differentiator
My Talking Man is not simply a virtual pet with a chatbot attached.
It is a customizable AI-powered 3D companion whose appearance, personality, communication style, interests, preferences, and conversational memory are controlled by the user.
The user should feel that they are interacting with a character that has a recognizable and consistent personality rather than a generic AI assistant.
The central experience combines three things:
1.	A personalized 3D boyfriend.
2.	An AI companion capable of discussing virtually any topic while maintaining a customized personality.
3.	An interactive virtual pet game with needs, activities, rewards, and customization.
